# StrataScratch Challenge 10127: Calculate Samantha's and Lisa's total sales revenue

**Source:** [View challenge](https://platform.stratascratch.com/coding/10127-calculate-samanthas-and-lisas-total-sales-revenue/submissions)

## Challenge

A sales operations team wants to verify the combined revenue generated by two of its salespeople. Find the total sales revenue generated by Samantha and Lisa.

Output the total sales revenue as a single number.

## SQL Solution #1

~~~sql
SELECT
    SUM(
        CASE 
            WHEN salesperson IN ('Samantha', 'Lisa')
            THEN sales_revenue
        END
    ) AS total_revenue
FROM sales_performance
;
~~~

_Dialect: MySQL_

## SQL Solution #2

~~~sql
SELECT
  SUM(sales_revenue) AS total_revenue
FROM sales_performance
WHERE salesperson IN ('Samantha', 'Lisa');
~~~

_Dialect: MySQL_
